# Evergrow — Level 2.1

[Download Level 2.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juahirhome-stack/arjuna1/evergrow-download/Evergrow-Level-2.1.zip)

Extract the ZIP, then open **Play-Evergrow.html** in Chrome or another modern desktop browser. No installation, account or Internet connection is needed to play.

Your first-person story starts in your bedroom. At **25 meters**, ten new houses and ten neighbors appear. Wave and check the neighborhood videos on your phone. At **100 meters**, a news helicopter arrives: wave to its camera and watch yourself on **Live news**. Four optional tasks track the chapter. All posts and news are fictional, drawn locally inside the game.

Choose **Quick** or **Super fast** growth pace to reach the chapters sooner. Growth pauses once near the ceiling, at 25 m, and at 100 m. Choose **Keep growing** when you are ready. Growth has no final size.

Controls: drag to look around, W A S D to walk, arrow keys or on-screen arrows to move and turn. Use the journal to explore your house. **Check my phone** unlocks at 25 m. Close the phone with its × button or Escape.

Validation: 26 unit tests and 10 browser tests cover the original story, exact chapter thresholds, optional tasks, animated phone pictures, offline gameplay, touch layouts, restart, and startup fallback behavior. Automated browser checks use Linux Chromium; the user's Windows Chrome and the chat Web preview are not directly tested.
