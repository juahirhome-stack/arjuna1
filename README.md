# Evergrow — Level 2.5

[Download Evergrow Level 2.5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juahirhome-stack/arjuna1/evergrow-download/Evergrow-Level-2.5.zip)

Extract the ZIP, then open **Play-Evergrow.html** in Chrome or another modern browser. It works offline, with no installer or account.

The same first-person growing story continues from home through 1 km and beyond:

- 25 m: ten houses, neighbors, and pretend social posts.
- 100 m: New York, news helicopter, skydivers, F1 magic boots, and the first rescues.
- 200–350 m: clouds, growing X-ray helmet, shielded NASA visitors, and more rescues.
- 400–500 m: floating buildings, office growth research, and a city celebration.
- 600 m: a world Morse-code call brings a friendly cosmic alien and the 828 m Burj Khalifa. Receive astronaut-suit pods and your self-replicating SRMAS.
- 800 m–1 km: alien scouts, suit-delivery helpers, new research readings, and unlimited continued growth.

Ten rescues have separate popularity rewards, pretend social posts, and saved replays. Arms stay a normal length. The skyline now reuses geometry and moves continuously instead of rebuilding every meter.

The ZIP contains only Play-Evergrow.html and READ-ME.txt. All news, social media, inventions, and air supplies are part of the pretend offline story. Session progress clears when the game closes.

Earlier downloads remain available in this branch. Browser checks use Linux Chromium; they do not directly test Windows Chrome or the chat's Web preview wrapper.
