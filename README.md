# Evergrow — Level 2

A pretend first-person growing adventure that begins inside your bedroom.

Download **Evergrow-Level-2.zip**, extract it, then open **Play-Evergrow.html** in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No installation, account or Internet connection is needed after downloading.

Choose **Begin your story**. Drag to look, use WASD or the on-screen arrows to move, and explore through the story journal. You can grow, pause or start over.

This branch contains the downloadable game and opening instructions.
