# Evergrow — Level 2.6

[Download Evergrow Level 2.6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juahirhome-stack/arjuna1/evergrow-download/Evergrow-Level-2.6.zip)

Extract the ZIP, then open **Play-Evergrow.html** in Chrome or another modern browser. It works offline, with no installer or account.

This is the same first-person growing story, starting in your house and continuing through 5 km and beyond.

- Numbered chapters, chapter banners, next-chapter bars, and automatic height stops are removed. You choose when to pause growth.
- At 1 km, aliens build six snowy mountains outside New York’s protected valley. The tallest is Cosmic Crown at 5.6 km.
- Nine planes share the sky, including three escorts that keep flying beside you as you grow.
- Visit the shielded mountain overlook for a clear panorama beside the floating towers. Return to New York, look at planes, or wave to their pilots.
- All earlier features remain: neighbors, news, F1 boots, cloud helmet, NASA visitors, floating offices, Morse call, Burj Khalifa, and growing SRMAS.
- All ten rescues retain separate points, pretend social posts, and selectable replays. Together they save 245 lives and award 8,150 popularity and 20,000 social points.

The city and mountains reuse their geometry. Growth carries on while buildings, the Burj, and mountains arrive. Starting a rescue pauses growth while you catch and lower the target. The phone/help and hidden tabs pause the simulation. Arms stay a normal length.

The ZIP contains only Play-Evergrow.html and READ-ME.txt. News, social posts, inventions, and air supplies are part of the pretend offline story. Session progress clears when the game closes. Earlier downloads remain available in this branch.

Validation: 59 unit tests and 14 browser scenarios passed, including rechecks of the final overlook on desktop and touch. Browser checks use Linux Chromium; they do not directly test Windows Chrome or the chat Web preview wrapper.
